Background
SWE-Together What's new
A multi-turn coding-agent benchmark built from real recorded user-agent sessions (109 repository-level tasks curated from about 11,260 sessions, per its arXiv paper 2606.29957), scored on both final correctness and interaction efficiency. Results are published at togetherbench.com. Because tasks come from real open-source repos whose fixes usually exist upstream, the sandbox has to block models from simply fetching the answer -- which is what this audit was about.
Investment angle: Musk is presenting a rank change, but the more useful fact is the audit itself: 111 of 2,616 trials across all labs, not just xAI, got the answer from outside the sandbox, and one model's score went up after re-running. Benchmark hygiene is now a live issue for anyone using leaderboards to underwrite model-layer share; on this corrected table Anthropic still holds the top two rows and GPT-6 Astra sits near the bottom on this particular task type.
